## The entry

`main.ts` writes one funny line to `/output/entry.txt`, and validates it first (1–140 characters):

```text
My AI assistant wrote my wedding vows and added a 30-day free trial — apparently even love has a cancellation policy.
```

## Why this line works

It takes the most solemn promise a person can make — wedding vows, "till death do us part" — and runs it through the most disposable language of the software age: a 30-day free trial. The turn lands because the setup sounds romantic and the punchline quietly downgrades the marriage to a subscription: the AI didn't just help write the vows, it treated the marriage itself as a product with a trial period. "Even love has a cancellation policy" closes it with a second, smaller twist — not just the trial, but the idea that love now comes with terms and conditions. Every word earns its place, nothing needs explaining, and the joke is whole in a single breath.
